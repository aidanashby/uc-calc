## Unverified

- **LHA freeze beyond 2026/27:** secondary sources (Big Issue, Crisis, NRLA) reported after the November 2025 Autumn Budget that the Local Housing Allowance and benefit cap freeze extends to April 2027, but I could not open the primary written ministerial statement to confirm this (parliament.uk's questions-statements site returned a 403 this session). The 2026/27 freeze itself is confirmed via gov.uk's "Benefit and pension rates 2026 to 2027". Worth another attempt at the December run, since it borders on affecting the footer's "hasn't risen since April 2024" wording once April 2027 is closer.
- **Healthy Start:** the UC earnings threshold that determines eligibility could not be confirmed against a primary source in a general-UC context this session (healthystart.nhs.uk was blocked by network egress). The plugin's own footer text doesn't state a threshold or amount, so no copy change is needed regardless. The April 2026 payment rate rises (£4.65/week for a child aged 1 to 4 or pregnant, £9.30/week for an infant under 1) were only found via an NHS Business Services Authority press release (secondary), not a gov.uk or legislation.gov.uk page.
- **Travel figure basis:** the current `travel_working` value (£22/week) matches First Bus's Weston-super-Mare weekly pass, not the Bristol/Bath weekly pass, which is £28. Both are confirmed current fares (unchanged since 4th January 2026), so this isn't a "figure is wrong" finding, just worth the maintainer double-checking which area the £22 figure is meant to reflect, since the info text says "for Bristol".

## Watch for future runs

- **January 2027 run (travel check):** the GB-wide single bus fare cap is confirmed to drop from £3 to £2 from 1st January 2027, running to 31st December 2027 ([gov.uk guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/3-national-bus-fare-cap), announced 22nd July 2026). First Bus's current Bristol/Bath/West single fare is £2.60, above the incoming £2 cap, so it will very likely have to drop by January. Re-check First Bus's actual fares then rather than now. The West of England Combined Authority's £1 child fare is unaffected and is confirmed frozen until Spring 2029 ([WECA announcement](https://www.westofengland-ca.gov.uk/news/families-set-to-save-millions-more-as-1-child-fares-frozen-in-west-until-2029/), 21st March 2026), matching the plugin's current `travel_child5to15` figure.
- **December 2026 run:** DWP is due to publish a newer UC deductions statistics release around 17th November 2026, which may move the percentage again before the figure proposed above is even applied. Also worth another attempt at confirming the LHA-freeze-to-2027 written statement (see Unverified, above).
